BEAUTIFUL LORD, WONDERFUL SAVIOUR
I KNOW FOR SURE
ALL OF MY DAYS ARE 
HELD IN MY HAND
CRAFTED INTO 
YOUR PERFECT PLAN

---

YOU GENTLY CALL ME
INTO YOUR PRESENCE
GUIDING ME BY
YOUR HOLY SPIRIT
TEACH ME DEAR LORD
TO LIVE ALL MY LIFE 
THROUGH YOUR EYES

---

I’M CAPTURED BY 
YOUR HOLY CALLING
SET ME APART
I KNOW YOU’RE DRAWING
ME TO YOURSELF
LEAD ME LORD I PRAY

---

TAKE ME, MOULD ME
USE ME, FILL ME
I GIVE MY LIFE TO THE POTTER’S HAND
CALL ME, GUIDE ME
LEAD ME, WALK BESIDE ME
I GIVE MY LIFE TO THE POTTERS HAND
