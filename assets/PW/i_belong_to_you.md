HERE I STAND
FOREVER IN YOUR MIGHTY HAND
LIVING WITH YOUR PROMISE
WRITTEN ON MY HEART

I AM YOURS
SURRENDERED WHOLLY TO YOU

CALLING ME YOUR OWN

HEAR MY CRY
MY DEEP DESIRE 
TO KNOW YOU MORE

WITH ALL I AM
I BELONG TO YOU
