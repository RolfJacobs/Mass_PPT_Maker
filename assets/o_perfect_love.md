O PERFECT LOVE
ALL HUMAN THOUGHT TRANSCENDING
LOWLY WE KNEEL
IN PRAYER BEFORE THY THRONE
THAT THEIRS MAY BE
THE LOVE WHICH KNOWS NO ENDING
WHOM THOU FOR EVERMORE
DOST JOIN IN ONE

---

O PERFECT LIFE
BE THOU THEIR FULL ASSURANCE
OF TENDER CHARITY
AND STEADFAST FAITH
OF PATIENT HOPE
AND QUIET, BRAVE ENDURANCE
WITH CHILDLIKE TRUST
THAT FEARS NOR PAIN OR DEATH

---

GRANT THEM THE JOY
WHICH BRIGHTENS EARTHLY SORROWS
GRANT THEM THE PEACE
WHICH CALMS ALL EARTHLY STRIFE
AND TO LIFE’S DAY
THE GLORIOUS UNKNOWN MORNINGS
THAT DAWNS UPON
ETERNAL LOVE AND LIFE
