THE TOWNSFOLK
I WANDERED IN THE HILLS AND ALL AROUND
I TRIED TO TELL MY FRIENDS 
ABOUT THE STORY

AND BABE I FOUND

BUT ALL ALONE I KNELT 
BEFORE THAT MANGER

STANDING BY
