HOLY VIRGIN BY GOD’S DECREE
YOU WERE CALLED ETERNALLY
THAT HE COULD GIVE HIS 
SON TO OUR RACE
MARY WE PRAISE YOU
HAIL FULL OF GRACE

AVE, AVE, AVE MARIA

---

BY YOUR FAITH AND LOVING ACCORD
AS THE HANDMAID OF THE LORD
YOU UNDERTOOK GOD’S 
PLAN TO EMBRACE
MARY WE THANK YOU
HAIL FULL OF GRACE

AVE, AVE, AVE MARIA

---

REFUGE FOR YOUR CHILDREN SO WEAK
SURE PROTECTION ALL CAN SEEK
PROBLEMS OF LIFE YOU HELP US TO FACE
MARY WE TRUST YOU
HAIL FULL OF GRACE

AVE, AVE, AVE MARIA

---

TO OUR NEEDY WORLD OF TODAY
LOVE AND BEAUTY YOU PORTRAY
SHOWING THE PATH TO 
CHRIST WE MUST TRACE
MARY OUR MOTHER
HAIL FULL OF GRACE

AVE, AVE, AVE MARIA
